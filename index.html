Our Roasting Secret

We roast our specialty beans using hot-air convection technology to preserve delicate floral notes.

Unlike traditional drum roasters that can scorch the outer bean surface, air-roasting caramelizes natural sugars evenly at 205°C. Every batch is cupped by certified baristas before serving at AITU!
