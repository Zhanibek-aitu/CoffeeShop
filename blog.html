How to Brew the Perfect Pour-Over at Home

Water Temperature and Grind Size

To extract the cleanest flavors, use water between 90°C and 96°C. A medium-fine grind size prevents bitter over-extraction while preserving fruity notes.

Understanding Your Coffee Beans

Arabica beans offer higher acidity and nuanced aroma, whereas Robusta carries higher caffeine content and a heavier body suitable for dark espresso blends.
